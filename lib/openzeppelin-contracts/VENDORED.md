The unmodified ERC20 dependency closure from OpenZeppelin Contracts **v5.0.2**
is vendored here as ordinary source files, including the upstream MIT license.

Source: https://github.com/OpenZeppelin/openzeppelin-contracts/tree/v5.0.2

Included: ERC20, IERC20, IERC20Metadata, Context, and draft-IERC6093.
No installation or network access is needed to build this project.
